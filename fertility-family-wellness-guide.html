Fertility & Family Wellness

Family planning is a shared journey. AYMP provides educational information about healthy routines and traditional Siddha wellness perspectives for couples.

Healthy foundations

Balanced nutrition, adequate sleep, regular physical activity, stress management and avoiding tobacco and excess alcohol are important foundations for reproductive health.

When to seek medical evaluation

If pregnancy has not occurred after an appropriate period of trying, or if either partner has symptoms or a known condition affecting fertility, professional medical evaluation is important. Traditional wellness practices should be complementary, not a replacement for fertility care.

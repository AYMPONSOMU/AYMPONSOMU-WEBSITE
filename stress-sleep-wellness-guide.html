Stress, Sleep & Nerve Wellness

Calm routines, regular sleep and healthy daily rhythms can support mental and physical well-being. AYMP shares traditional wellness perspectives for educational use.

Simple daily practices

Keep a consistent sleep schedule, reduce stimulating screen use before bed, stay physically active, make time for relaxation and maintain supportive social connections.

When to seek help

Severe anxiety, persistent insomnia, new neurological symptoms, confusion, fainting or thoughts of self-harm require prompt professional help. Wellness practices should never delay appropriate care.

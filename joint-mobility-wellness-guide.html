Joint & Mobility Wellness

Healthy movement and sensible daily routines can support mobility and quality of life. This page shares general wellness information and traditional Siddha perspectives.

Daily habits

Gentle movement, appropriate strengthening, healthy body weight, adequate sleep and safe activity progression can be useful foundations for joint wellness.

Persistent pain

New, severe or persistent joint pain, swelling, weakness, injury or difficulty walking should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional. Do not delay appropriate medical care because of traditional remedies.

Metabolic Wellness

Metabolic health is supported by consistent lifestyle habits. AYMP shares educational wellness information that can complement, but not replace, professional care.

Healthy foundations

Regular physical activity, balanced meals, adequate sleep, healthy weight management and following a clinician's monitoring plan are important foundations.

Diabetes and blood pressure

People with diabetes, high blood pressure or other diagnosed conditions should continue prescribed medicines and monitoring. Do not stop or change treatment because of information on this website. Seek medical advice for unusual readings or symptoms.

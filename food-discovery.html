INDIA • TRADITIONAL

Vegetable Biryani

A fragrant rice dish prepared with vegetables, rice and aromatic spices.

👨‍🍳 Ingredients & Preparation
Ingredients: Basmati rice, mixed vegetables, onion, tomato, ginger-garlic, herbs and spices.
Method: Prepare the spiced vegetable base, add soaked rice and water, then cook until the rice is tender. Adjust seasoning to taste.
🥐
FRANCE • BAKERY

Croissant

A layered pastry known for its crisp exterior and buttery interior.

👨‍🍳 Ingredients & Preparation
Ingredients: Flour, yeast, milk, butter, sugar and salt.
Method: Prepare and chill the dough, layer it with butter, fold and roll several times, shape into crescents, proof and bake until golden.
🍣
JAPAN • TRADITIONAL

Sushi

Seasoned rice combined with a variety of fresh or cooked ingredients.

👨‍🍳 Ingredients & Preparation
Ingredients: Sushi rice, rice vinegar, nori and suitable fillings or toppings.
Method: Cook and season the rice, cool it, then shape it with nori and chosen fillings. Serve with suitable accompaniments.
